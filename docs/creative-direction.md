# ASHKE — Creative Direction

**Song:** Ashke, Karan Aujla × Mxrci (Aujla SZN 1) · 217.8 s · 136 BPM
**Deliverables:** full lyric video 1920×1080 (16:9, like the official MV) and a 45 s Reel 1080×1920 (9:16, song 47.15 s to 92.31 s, the same window as the reference reel).

## 1. Concept: "The Order of the Day"

The official video drops a Punjabi Jatt into an English country estate: a butler in white gloves, clay shooting, polo, afternoon tea, Aston Martins, a river launch, a Patek in a champagne coupe, and a family portrait that takes over the lawn. Old money, new owner.

The lyric video treats that day as a printed **country-house programme**, the kind of letterpressed card a stately home hands its guests. Every section of the song is a numbered item on the programme (I. The Arrival … XII. Jhumar). The typography behaves like ink, gold foil, wax and engraved brass that lands on top of the film. The swagger comes from the contrast between the restrained British stationery and the Punjabi lines that run through it.

This is a lyric video *built on* the film, not a subtitle track over it. The people stay on screen the whole time. The design reacts to what they do and to what the line means.

## 2. Three worlds

| World | Where | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **BONE** (stationery) | title, invitation, postcard, placards, chapter cards | Cream paper `#EFE6D2`, letterpress black ink `#16130F`, engraved hairlines |
| **ESTATE** (daylight film) | shoot, polo, tea, motors, river | Graded footage (warm shadows, cool highlights, grain, vignette); bone and gold type |
| **OXBLOOD** (night and finale) | the study, the vault, dinner, the jhumar hook | Deep brown-black, oxblood `#6E1F24` (the boat's leather), heavy gold `#D4AE5C` |

## 3. Signature device: the gold hairline

One continuous 2 px gold line is the film's "character", like the red line in the PYAAR? guide. It:
- draws the invitation border and the title underline,
- becomes the **active-word underline** that slides from word to word as Karan raps,
- turns into the **gun-sight** reticle on "फट",
- draws the **picture frame** around the family portrait and the oil painting ("photo भी नी पाई"),
- becomes the **watch dial** in the vault, the **snake** on "नाग", and the **jhumar circle** in the finale.

## 4. Typography system

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lyric lines | Tiro Devanagari Hindi | Book serif, editorial. Words are revealed by word timestamps, never split into Unicode code points, so matras and conjuncts stay intact. |
| Hero words | Rozha One | High-contrast Devanagari display face, gold foil gradient |
| English code-switches | Cormorant Garamond *Italic* | "trust", "Baby you so fire", "Lambo", "photo"… The code-switch is visible in the type |
| Programme and labels | Cormorant Garamond SC tracking + IBM Plex Mono | Chapter cards, placards, data ("22 GENTLEMEN · 23 GUNS · 12 CARS") |
| Handwriting | Kalam | The postcard (the female line "वे मैं miss करदी आ तिनू") |

Lyrics sit left-aligned in the lower third inside a 2.39:1 letterbox, not centred. Hero words break the grid and bleed.

## 5. Motion principles

- **Paper** settles with a slow damped rotation. **Wax** hits hard: fast fall, squash, rebound, a ring of dust. **Gold** draws and shimmers, never bounces. **Ink** rises out of a baseline mask.
- Words rise out of a mask on their timestamp with a short overshoot (no linear slides, no scale-from-zero). The past line lifts away with a stagger.
- Beat data (`beat-map.json`) drives secondary motion only: letterbox breathing on downbeats, hero-word impacts, camera nudges. Not everything pulses.

## 7. Twelve moments

1. **Title (0 s):** the gold hairline engraves the programme border; KARAN AUJLA × MXRCI in small caps.
2. **Arrival:** mono labels over the butler opening the Aston: "ARRIVAL · 16:12", "12 CARS · 22 GENTLEMEN · 23 GUNS" (a nod to the MV's own dialogue).
3. **The invitation (24 s):** a letterpress card slides in and settles: *The Estate requests the pleasure of your company*, plus the Order of the Day. It is the table of contents for the whole film.
4. **अश्के title (31 s):** giant Rozha One in gold foil over the slow-motion estate. The underline becomes the horizon.
5. **"Oh-oh" (38 s):** the vocal is drawn as one flowing gold melodic line across the frame.
6. **"Mercy" (45.7 s):** an oxblood wax seal stamps the frame (impact, dust, camera shake). The verse drops at 48.2 s.
7. **"फट" (63.7 s):** the gold reticle tracks Karan's shotgun; on the MV's own shot the frame flashes and the clay bursts into shards.
8. **"पिंडां आळियां" (84 s):** a Punjabi village milestone (white stone, yellow cap) rises into the English garden: *पिंड · 0 km*.
9. **The letter (102 s):** a postcard with stamp and postmark "LONDON ↔ PUNJAB"; the woman on the river, the line handwritten.
10. **The portrait (106.6 s):** gold frame around the whole family; museum placards read नानके — *simple*, दादके — *योग्य*.
11. **"photo भी नी पाई" (133 s):** a shutter flash and an empty gilt frame, "0 posts", while Karan stands under the real oil portrait.
12. **Finale (189.8 s):** अश्के three times, each bigger, inside a turning jhumar ring of gold dots. On the last one the type fills the frame.

## 8. Self-review: rejected ideas

- Hearts or fire emojis on "Baby you so fire": replaced with a few warm embers and an italic line.
- Re-using the reticle for "असला" (arms): it would repeat the "फट" moment, so it was cut.
- A glitch transition anywhere: off-brand for old money; the cuts belong to the film.
- Full-screen chapter cards between every section: they would interrupt the edit, so chapters are small engraved corner labels instead.
