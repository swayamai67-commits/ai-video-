# ASHKE: "EYES ON THE JATT" (viral cut)

**Song:** Ashke, Karan Aujla × Mxrci · 217.8 s · 136 BPM
**Deliverable:** full-song vertical edit, 1080×1920 at 25 fps, for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.
**Lyrics:** romanized Punjabi in English letters, with the original timing unchanged (`docs/viral/lyrics-romanized.md`). Only 13 words switch to Punjabi script (Gurmukhi), and only where the word carries the song: ਅਸ਼ਕੇ, ਜੱਟ, ਪਿੰਡ/ਪਿੰਡਾਂ, ਯਾਰ, ਸੀਨਾ, ਬਾਬੇ, ਡਾਕਾ, ਨਾਗ.

This is a different film from the first cut (the letterpress *Order of the Day* in `docs/creative-direction.md`). That one was old-money stationery; this one is the internet.

## 1. Concept

In the official video a Jatt walks into an English country estate and everyone watches him: the butler, the gentlemen with shotguns, the woman on the boat, the family on the lawn. The song is about being watched and still not posting a photo ("haale photo bhi ni paayi"), about enemies who look at you sideways ("tu takdi tedhi tedhi"), and about the crowd shouting *Ashke!* (bravo).

So the edit **is the feed**: estate CCTV crossed with a viral fan edit. Tracking boxes lock onto him, feature points chase every move, stickers of him slap onto paper, and the type slams on the beat. When the hook arrives, the surveillance grid breaks into a **phulkari** burst: Punjabi embroidery geometry tearing through the English frame.

## 2. Three worlds

| World | Where | Look |

## 3. Signature device: the lock

A red corner-bracket box (the **lock**) is the film's character, like the red line in the PYAAR? guide. It locks onto Karan's head in the arrival, labels people (TARGET, HOST, NAANKE, DAADKE), frames the active word, becomes the crosshair on "phatt", the WANTED stamp on "daaka", and finally bursts into the phulkari diamond on every *Ashke*.

## 5. Lyric systems (meaning first)

- **SLAM**: one word at a time, huge, snapping in with scale and blur and leaving on a hard cut. The phrase so far ticks along above it in mono. Used for the fast verse.
- **LOCKUP**: the phrase is set in rows fitted to the full width (each row scaled to the frame), revealed word by word, with the active word in red. Used for the hooks and the slower lines.
- **BEHIND**: a giant word sits *behind* the person cutout (text-behind-subject).
- **Meaning moves** (never generic zoom/slide):
  - *kalla* (alone): the word goes tiny, alone in black.
  - *putthiyan raahan* (reverse roads): the word flips upside down.
  - *ghisda* (dragging): the word smears sideways.
  - *neeli chhatt* (the blue sky): the word rises into sky blue.
  - *kaale* (black): the frame inverts.
  - *laaliyan* (blush): the word turns red.
  - *chauda seena* (broad chest): the word stretches wide.
  - *tedhi tedhi* (sideways looks): the word tilts left, then right.
  - *phatt* (gunshot): crosshair, flash, RGB split and a bullet crack.
  - *phaad ke* (breaking through stone): the frame cracks.
  - *naag* (snake): a line slithers through the text and is cut.
  - *photo bhi ni paayi* (haven't even posted a photo): an empty post that reads "0 posts".
  - *Ve main miss kardi aa tainu*: the female line arrives as a chat message, with typing dots and "Seen".

## 7. Self-review: rejected ideas

- **Emoji rain on "fire"**: cheap. Replaced with italic type, heat and a few embers.
- **Glitch on every cut**: it would numb the hits. RGB split is kept for *phatt*, *Mercy* and the finale only.
- **Full-line karaoke subtitles**: the "AI subtitle" look. Replaced with SLAM, LOCKUP and BEHIND.
- **The old render's moments** (wax seal, milestone, postcard, gilt frame): they belong to the first cut. Their burned-in pixels are cropped out of every plate here.

## 8. Footage note

The only footage in this repository is the first cut's 16:9 render (`out/ashke-full-16x9-web.mp4`), whose picture is the official MV in song time. Every plate here is a crop window chosen to stay clear of that render's burned-in lyrics and overlays (`alignment/viral_plates.py`). The plates are therefore softer than a clean 4K source. Grain, halftone and grading are used so that softness reads as style. Supply the clean MV and only the crop table needs to change.
