# EARRINGS · creative direction

**Song:** "Earrings" by Malcolm Todd (33.4 s reel cut). **Format:** 9:16, rendered natively at 2160×3840, 60 fps.

## Concept: "The One That Got Away"

The song is about a small object that holds a big secret: you left your earrings in her bed, you're too scared to say so, and the silence pushes you apart. So the video has **one hero object, a single gold hoop earring**, and it is the only gold thing in the film. It drops in at frame 1 and keeps changing role: an earring on your ear, a thing lost in the sheets, a glint in the dark, the circle of a "typing…" bubble, the line that tries to draw a heart and gives up, one of a pair drifting apart, and finally the **O of "Oh."**

People are drawn as **continuous-line illustrations**, the hand-drawn, one-line style that is all over 2025–26 lyric edits. The lines "boil" at 12 fps like hand-animated cels, even though the film runs at 60 fps.

## Visual worlds (risograph, two inks on paper)

| World | Where | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| **PAPER** (day, the thinking) | head, bed, phone, heart | warm paper `#F3EBDD`, Midnight ink `#1F2547`, Fluoro Pink `#FF4F7B` |
| **NIGHT** (the silence) | back-to-back in bed, "scared", "not talking" | Midnight ground, paper-colour line, pink kept for *her* |
| **FLOOD** (the release) | "Oh." | pink flood, Midnight type, gold hoop |

Gold `#E8B04A` belongs **only** to the earring. *Her* is always drawn in pink and *you* in Midnight, so you can tell who is who without faces. Print imperfections: grain, halftone fills, and a 5 px pink misregistration offset on fills.

## Typography

- **Instrument Serif** (regular and italic) for hero words: editorial, intimate, very 2026.
- **Space Grotesk 700** for small caps connective lyrics.
- **Caveat** for handwriting: thoughts and drafts.
- **DM Mono** for UI and print-slug details.

Rule: one hero word per line can take over the frame. The rest stays small. Nothing is centred twice in a row.

## Motion principles

- The line draws itself; it never fades in. Ink **writes**, metal **swings** (pendulum spring), paper **tears**, tape **slaps** (fast in, squash, settle).
- Words are timed to the frozen word timestamps; beats (≈80.7 BPM, half-time 161) only drive secondary motion: a camera nudge on downbeats and glints.
- Every scene change has a motive: the camera goes through the hoop, the lights go out, the phone screen fills, the hoop unwinds into a line, the paper tears, the hoop becomes the O.
