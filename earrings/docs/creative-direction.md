# EARRINGS — creative direction

**Song:** "Earrings", Malcolm Todd (from the mixtape *Sweet Boy*, 2024; went viral on TikTok in 2026 and became a Columbia single).
**Clip:** 0:00 → 0:33.4, the first verse plus the held "Oh… from you" tag.
**Format:** 9:16 Reel. Designed at 1080×1920 and rendered at **2160×3840 (4K UHD vertical), 60 fps**. Everything is vector or 4K-native, so it is rendered at 4K rather than upscaled.

## What the song is actually saying

This is not a cheating song or a breakup song. It is about **silence**. Someone left a pair of earrings in a bed. They are too scared to text and ask for them back. So the earrings stay there, the message stays in drafts, and the two people drift apart. The lost earrings are the excuse to reach out that never gets used.

The music is soft bedroom R&B: about 161 BPM (it feels like half-time, about 80), a warm guitar and keys loop, close falsetto vocals, and steady energy from 0:01 to 0:26 that falls away into a long held "Oh". The visuals should be **intimate, late-night and restrained**, not loud. The loud moments are small and rare: a glint, a drop, a typing bubble that disappears.

## Concept: "Left on Read"

**One gold hoop earring is the main character.** It plays the part the red line plays in the reference guide: the visual thread that never breaks. It changes role scene by scene:

| Lyric | The hoop becomes… |
|---|---|
| intro | a glint that spins into existence (a 3D Y-axis spin) |
| "Her **love** is in your head" | the **o** in *love*, then a thought orbiting inside a one-line drawing of a head |
| "You lost your **earrings** in her bed" | it falls out of the head and lands as a **pair** on a silk sheet, then the folds swallow them |
| "You couldn't tell her…" | her contact photo in a chat thread (the phone was lying on the bed) |
| "…save it for another day" | the "saving to drafts" spinner |
| "never had the **heart**" | the gold wire draws a heart that **never closes** |
| "they just **drift** further **apart**" | the heart splits into its two lobes, which become two hoops drifting apart in the dark |
| "**Oh**…" | the last hoop becomes the giant **O** |

## The worlds

1. **HEAD (0:00–0:03.5).** Deep indigo night with a rose-coloured glow. A one-line profile drawn in thin cream strokes, like editorial one-line art.
2. **SHEET (0:03.5–0:06.5).** Ivory silk sheets seen from above, lit by a soft window light. This is the only bright, warm daylight world in the film. The camera reaches it by **falling with the earrings**.
3. **THREAD (0:06.5–0:18.3).** Full-bleed iMessage-style dark mode. The frame *is* the phone screen. Cold blue bubble light. Texting is the most recognisable visual language in viral lyric edits, so it carries the "not talking" verse. Lyrics are typed, deleted, drafted and saved. The camera reaches this world by **pushing into the shadow of a sheet fold**, where a phone screen lights up.
4. **WIRE (0:18.3–0:21.4).** The phone locks and goes to black glass. Only the gold wire and the serif type remain. This is the deliberate minimal moment before the drift.
5. **DRIFT (0:21.4–0:28).** Deep space. Bokeh dust, two hoops and the camera slowly pulling back. Letter spacing literally drifts apart.
6. **OH (0:28–0:33.4).** One hoop becomes the O. It glows with gold halation, then dims to a single glint, followed by the title card.

## Typography system

| Role | Face | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / emotional | **Instrument Serif** *(italic and roman)* | The editorial serif look that dominates 2025–26 indie lyric edits. Hero words run large, at 160–420 px. |
| UI / diegetic | **Inter Tight** | Chat bubbles, input field, toasts, contact name. |
| Data | **JetBrains Mono** | Timestamps, the date counter, the title card. |
| Hand | **Caveat** | Strike-throughs and draft scribbles (sparingly). |
| Punch | **Anton** | Used **once**: the word SCARED. |

Rules: no centred-subtitle look. Most lines are set off-axis, hero words dominate, and function words stay tiny. Reels safe zone: critical text stays between y 240 and 1500 and x 60 to 900. Only decoration and oversized type bleed past it.

## Colour

`#0A0B16` midnight · `#14162B` indigo · `#D9A1A0` dusk rose · `#EFE6DA` ivory · **gold** `#F6DE9C → #D9A956 → #8C6224` (metal gradient) · `#2F7BFF` iMessage blue (used only inside THREAD) · `#FF4D4F` "Not Delivered" red (used once).

## Motion principles

- **Metal is light and hard.** The hoops fall with gravity, land with small, fast-decaying bounces and finish with a specular glint. They never squash.
- **Silk is slow.** Folds breathe and shadows sweep. Nothing snaps on the sheet.
- **UI is digital.** Typing goes character by character with a blinking caret. Backspace runs faster than typing (panic). Bubbles pop in with a slight overshoot.
- **Wire draws.** It never fades in; it is always stroked on.
- **Drift is inertial.** It is linear-ish with a slight ease and never stops.
- **Camera:** a virtual 2.5D camera with a subtle handheld drift (seeded noise). It tilts down with the falling earrings, pushes into the fold, and pulls back during the drift.
- **Beat use:** the kick map drives only a slight exposure pulse and grain shift, plus a 1–2% punch-in on the 4 biggest moments (*earrings*, *SCARED*, *heart*, *Oh*). It does **not** pulse on every beat.

## Ten key moments

1. A glint spins up into a gold hoop, and it becomes the "o" in ***love***.
2. A one-line head draws itself around the words, and the hoop orbits inside it like a thought.
3. On "You lost", the hoop drops out of the head and the camera **falls with it** onto the silk sheet. Two hoops land on *earrings* with hard metallic bounces.
4. On *bed*, a fold shadow slides over the pair and the camera pushes into the dark crease, where a phone lights up.
5. "You couldn't tell her that you lost them" is **typed** into the message field to her ("her" with a gold-hoop avatar). On "'cause you're scared", it is backspaced in panic.
6. **SCARED** in Anton shakes, and her typing bubble "…" appears. On *not talking*, it disappears. A red "Not Delivered" appears under nothing.
7. "So you think of what to say": ghost draft bubbles appear and are struck through one by one.
8. On "save it", the bubble collapses into a drafts spinner (the hoop). On "another day", the date stamp flips day after day, and the screen times out to black.
9. The gold wire draws a heart around ***heart*** and stops just short of closing.
10. The heart splits into two hoops that drift apart. "a p a r t" tears in two, "from you." is left alone in the dark, and on **Oh** one hoop rushes into the frame to become the O.
